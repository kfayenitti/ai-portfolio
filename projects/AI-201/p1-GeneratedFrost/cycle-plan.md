# Cycle Plan


## Write your Cycle Plan

### 1) What do I intend to build?

> I will be creating a frost simulation in Houdini, assisted by AI tools. The final output will be a Houdini render composited together in Nuke, also assisted with generative AI for ideation.


### 2) What is my First Playable target?

> Frost simulation working with customizable controls.

### 3) What tools am I using?

> ClaudeAI, ComfyUI.

### 4) What is my timeline for the next two weeks?

> **Make**

A VFX visual of frost in Houdini, assisted by AI generated code in Claude, and visually assisted with generated imagery in ComfyUI.

**Week 03**

Research and troubleshooting.

Find practitioners and similar projects. Begin to use research to generate the frost tool and troubleshooting.

**Week 04**

Development of frost generation tool. Tool should be functional by mid-week, to allow for application of more customization options.

Has adjustable branching, scalable, location/geo-customizable.

**Week 05**

Finish AI-coded frost tool. The frost should be functional and easy to art direct in Houdini.

**Week 06**

Progress down the pipeline. Now having a tool, experiment with how I can continue to use AI to create a final product.

Render with AI? Lighting iterations with AI? AI assisted matte painting in the bg? Playblast preview renders? Assess while working the most realistic applications.

**Week 07**

Utilize generative AI to assist in generating ideations for lighting and composition. Compare side-by-side different outputs and Houdini image.

**Week 08**

Utilize generative AI to assist in compositing and ideation. Compare side-by-side different outputs and Houdini image.

**Week 09**

Finish the final product, ensuring adhered to project intentions.

**Week 10**

Adjustments based on feedback.
